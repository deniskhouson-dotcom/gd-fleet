AI Fleet Drive Sync

A private, single-user automation tool.

AI Fleet Drive Sync is a personal application operated by its owner to automate file handling on the owner's own Google Drive. It uploads generated reports and documents to the owner's Drive and reads the owner's own files for local processing. It is not a multi-user product and is not offered to the public.

How it works

  • Authenticates to Google Drive via Google OAuth, using the drive scope, on the owner's own Google account only.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own machine and are never shared.
  • No third party receives any data; there is no analytics or tracking.

Privacy

Privacy Policy — AI Fleet Drive Sync

Last updated: 15 October 2026

AI Fleet Drive Sync ("the app") is a private, single-user automation tool operated by its owner. This policy explains how it handles data.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by its owner, authenticating with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to, or used by, any other person.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's explicit Google OAuth consent (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive), the app reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive solely to: upload files and reports the owner generates, and read files the owner selects for local processing. The app does not access any other person's data.

How data is stored and shared

  • OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is transmitted to, sold to, or shared with any third party.
  • The app performs no advertising, profiling, analytics, or tracking.
  • File contents are processed locally and are not sent to any external service by the app.

Limited Use disclosure

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and revocation

Tokens persist locally until deleted by the owner. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the app can no longer access the owner's Drive.
